# Openfront Dental

The open-source alternative to Dentrix.

Openfront Dental runs a dental practice on one system: patients and families, scheduling by operatory and provider, tooth and perio charting, treatment plans, insurance eligibility, claims and remittance posting, patient balances, payment plans and memberships, recall and hygiene, lab cases, consent forms, the practice's public website with online booking, and the patient portal. Built-in AI agents can verify insurance, chase claims and fill the schedule through MCP tools.

Built on Next.js and KeystoneJS 6 with PostgreSQL.

## Status

The foundation is being built. The data model and build plan are in progress.
